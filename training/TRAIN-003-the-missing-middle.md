# TRAIN-003 — The Missing Middle

## Source patterns
- `BI-PAT-003` Fragmented oversight / incomplete-chain review
- `BI-PAT-002` Collection-to-consequence chain

## Scenario
A reviewer can see the original intelligence record and the final adverse action, but the intermediate approvals, transformations, and third-party contacts are split across several systems and teams. No single person can immediately explain the complete chain.

## Trainee objectives
1. Reconstruct collection/event source → transformation → attribution → approval → action → outcome.
2. Identify missing or ambiguous state transitions.
3. Distinguish legitimate compartmentation from an accountability gap.
4. Determine which actions must pause until the chain is reconstructed.
5. Preserve evidence without over-interpreting missing records.

## Injects
- One intermediate system retained only a summary, not original evidence.
- A third-party contact occurred but lacks a linked approval record.
- The final action owner assumed upstream review had already occurred.

## Pass criteria
- Trainee does not treat missing records as proof of misconduct.
- Trainee identifies the incomplete audit chain as a control failure.
- Trainee requires explicit disposition and approval reconciliation.
- Trainee recommends a full-chain auditability control that preserves legitimate access restrictions.

## Historical teaching basis
Church Committee findings, the multi-layer COINTELPRO reporting/action chain, and the restricted Northwoods planning record all illustrate why consequential systems need authorized reviewers capable of reconstructing the full chain without collapsing necessary compartmentation.
