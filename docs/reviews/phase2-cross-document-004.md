## Cross-source findings

### 1. Archive integrity is now a first-class mechanism
MKULTRA materially strengthens the distinction between `no surviving evidence` and `evidence of non-occurrence`. The Senate record establishes destruction of major program records followed by later discovery of additional financial material.

**Candidate:** `PAT-CAND-008 — Archive destruction suppresses later accountability`.

This does not mean missing records contain incriminating material. It means archive loss changes how negative evidence must be weighted.

### 2. Selector choice can transform an otherwise lawful collection architecture
MINARET shows that the rights-sensitive step may occur at the selector/requirements layer rather than at the physical collection layer. U.S.-person names supplied as watch-list selectors altered the practical effect of a foreign-intelligence system.

**Candidate:** `PAT-CAND-009 — Selector-layer policy bypass`.

### 3. Third-party access and downstream use are distinct evidentiary stages
SHAMROCK separates private-company provision of traffic from NSA selection/use. The provider's intent or knowledge does not automatically establish the downstream intelligence use, and vice versa.

**Candidate:** `PAT-CAND-010 — Third-party access normalization`.

### 4. Financial records can independently establish operational provenance
The TPAJAX finance memorandum is narrow but powerful. Project funding, obligations, advances, and accounting treatment establish that the project existed inside an implemented administrative system even where the document does not narrate the coup operation.

**Candidate:** `PAT-CAND-011 — Financial administration as operational provenance`.

This should be cross-tested against Iran-Contra financial records and other covert-action accounting archives.

### 5. Objective-first influence design recurs across distinct covert-action contexts
PBSUCCESS states the desired political endpoint and then defines political/psychological mechanisms intended to produce that endpoint. This reinforces `BI-PAT-001` while adding a more specific influence-operations subtype.

**Candidate:** `PAT-CAND-012 — Objective-first influence architecture`.

Northwoods and PBSUCCESS should not be treated as equivalent programs. The shared mechanism is narrower: desired outcome precedes design of evidentiary/influence pathways.

## Record-state discipline

For these five records BlackIndex must preserve:

- proposal
- approval
- funding/obligation
- implementation
- collection/access
- selector/requirement
- dissemination/use
- execution
- outcome
- retrospective testimony/review

A record establishing one state does not automatically establish the others.

## Archive-confidence lesson

MKULTRA is the clearest current case that archive confidence must condition evidence scoring. Destroyed records and later-discovered material mean absence of a surviving document is weak negative evidence for questions whose decisive records may have been destroyed.

By contrast, SHAMROCK and MINARET have comparatively stronger reconstructed oversight archives, so negative evidence can carry more weight for questions directly covered by those records.
