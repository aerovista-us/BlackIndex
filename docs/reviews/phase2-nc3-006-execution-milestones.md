## 95th Wing organizational execution

The AFGSC article reports that the 95th Wing officially activated on **28 Feb 2025** under Program Action Directive 24-03; the public ceremony/report followed on 28 Mar 2025. The wing integrates previously disaggregated command-and-control units and is charged with assured C2/C3 functions for strategic forces.

The article says the wing is **expected** to reach full operational capability in 2027. That remains a forecast in this source and is not encoded as completed.
