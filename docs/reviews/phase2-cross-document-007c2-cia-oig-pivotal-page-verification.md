## Verified finding set

### 1. Scope / source dependence

Pages v and vii state that the accountability review focused on Joint Inquiry findings relating to CIA, was not a comprehensive review of all Agency counterterrorism capabilities, and did not reach conclusions about other agencies or their personnel.

Durable object: `IR-2005-cia-oig-scope-joint-inquiry-dependence`.

### 2. Law / misconduct negative finding

Page vi states that the Team found no instance of an employee violating the law and says the errors discussed did not involve misconduct. The same page separately frames the review around unsatisfactory performance and accountability standards.

Durable object: `IR-2005-cia-oig-no-law-violation-misconduct-finding`.

This is a scoped negative finding, not a finding that performance was satisfactory.

### 3. No single point / systemic failures balance

Page vii states both that the Team found neither a single point of failure nor a silver bullet that would have enabled prediction/prevention of the attacks, and that it found failures in process implementation/management, operational follow-through, and sharing/analyzing critical data.

Durable object: `IR-2005-cia-oig-no-single-point-systemic-failures`.

The two parts must travel together in later synthesis.

### 4. Hazmi / Mihdhar watchlisting and information sharing

Pages xiii–xvi describe problems in two related processes: watchlisting and passing information to FBI channels. The Executive Summary says officers did not timely recommend watchlisting Hazmi and Mihdhar, describes broad readership of relevant cables, and characterizes the watchlisting program as lacking coherent functioning.

Durable object: `IR-2005-cia-oig-hazmi-mihdhar-watchlisting-breakdown`.

This is evidence of an OIG institutional/process finding. It is not by itself evidence of intent, advance knowledge of the 9/11 plot, or misconduct.

### 5. FBI receipt uncertainty

Pages xiv–xv describe a January 2000 message that was drafted but never sent and another cable saying information had been passed. The OIG Team states it could not confirm that the information was passed or that it was not passed, while finding no indication that CTC checked to ensure FBI receipt.

Durable object: `IR-2005-cia-oig-fbi-receipt-uncertainty`.

The uncertainty is part of the evidence and must not be silently resolved in either direction.

## Accountability recommendations

Pages xiv and xvi recommend Accountability Board review of specified management performance relating to watchlisting and operational follow-through. Names/identifying material are redacted in the public Executive Summary.

BlackIndex records these as recommendations for performance review, not as findings of criminality or misconduct.

## Review-process caveat

A footnote on printed page xiii states that, because of a conflict of interest, the Inspector General recused himself from deliberations concerning Agency components/individuals relating to the KSM issue and strategic-analysis issues; it also describes non-participation by two successive Deputy Inspectors General in accountability discussions regarding analysis and other issues.

This is preserved as a process caveat. BlackIndex does not infer from the recusal alone that the findings are invalid, independent, biased, or unbiased.
