## What the directive itself establishes

The publication is a mandatory Air Force Global Strike Command instruction. It states that it implements AFPD 13-5 and AFI 13-530 and defines roles, responsibilities, and minimum requirements for ALCS mission-ready evaluation programs for Minuteman III employment.

This makes the directive a primary governing record for the 2017 evaluation framework. It is not merely a retrospective institutional history.

## Authority/authentication clues actually present

Paragraph 4.1.2.5 requires applicable EWO, positive-control, code-control, alert, and safety directives to be available for evaluation scenarios. The cited family includes AFI 91-117, AFGSCI 13-5304, EAP-STRAT Volumes 1/3/8/11/16, and related directives.

Attachment 6 distinguishes evaluation conditions based on whether MCC-A has authenticated an execution order and repeatedly defers wartime-commit definitions to AFGSCI 13-5302V4.

The directive therefore establishes that execution-order authentication and wartime-commit procedures are real procedural dependencies of ALCS crew evaluation in this 2017 framework.

It does **not** provide the complete authority chain or reproduce the full controlling procedures.

## Classification decision

The preserved directive is classified `canon` at the **document level**.

That means the official artifact, its text, its date, its scope, and its explicit references are sufficiently established to serve as the current BlackIndex baseline.

It does **not** mean that:

- every referenced publication is already recovered;
- a citation imports the unseen contents of that publication;
- 2017 procedures can be projected backward onto 1960s LOOKING GLASS operations;
- crew evaluation authority equals nuclear-use authorization;
- capability or training proves operational use.

The referenced authority/source family remains a separate unresolved missing-evidence object.

## Acquisition rule

Recovery is limited to authorized public, official, or declassified first-party copies. Some cited publications may be controlled or classified. BlackIndex should record that condition rather than seek or substitute unauthorized copies.

Unofficial mirrors may assist discovery only; they do not become the preserved source artifact when first-party provenance is unavailable.
