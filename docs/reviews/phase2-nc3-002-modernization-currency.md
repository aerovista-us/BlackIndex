## Public strategic / modernization findings

The 2022 NPR defines five high-level nuclear command-and-control functions and states that next-generation NC3 modernization must emphasize resilience, warning/assessment, command-post and communications links, decision support, and integrated planning/operations.
The 2025 USSTRATCOM statement uses a Detect / Decide / Direct framework to describe the public NC3 mission model, while identifying legacy sustainment and modernization priorities including SAOC, E-130J TACAMO, satellite communications, warning/tracking, ground NC3, and cyber/data protection.

The 2026 statement continues that public institutional picture and adds an explicit NC3 Enterprise Center description plus replacement of the E-6B Airborne Command Post / LOOKING GLASS among the named modernization priorities.

These annual statements are longitudinal institutional reporting. They are not independent corroboration of the underlying program records, requirements, controlled directives, or actual operational use.
