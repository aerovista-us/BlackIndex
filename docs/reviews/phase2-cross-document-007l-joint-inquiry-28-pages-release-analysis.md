## Why “28 pages” is not the PDF page count

Visual inspection of the preserved official HPSCI artifact establishes:

- physical pages **1–4**: January 29, 2003 transmittal letter to CIA Director George Tenet;
- physical page **5**: Joint Inquiry report title page;
- physical pages **6–34**: scans of the original Part Four page family;
- first Part Four scan page: original page **415**;
- last Part Four scan page: original page **443**.

Representative visual checks confirm the internal sequence:

The familiar label is therefore a historical/conventional label, not a literal statement that the released PDF contains 28 physical pages.

## Relationship to the current GovInfo parent

The GovInfo parent’s Part Four spans physical pages **427–454**, public pages **395–422**. Those public pages carry bracketed original-page markers covering the same original classified-page family, **415–443**, but the public rendering consolidates some original page boundaries.

This matters because the two artifacts are **not merely byte-identical PDFs with different black bars**:

- pagination differs;
- page composition differs;
- the 2016 file includes transmittal/title material;
- the 2016 Part Four pages are scans of the original-page family;
- the current GovInfo public-report rendering uses public report pagination and bracketed/redacted text;
- wording visible in the 2016 release is materially more specific in places.

For example, the first Part Four page in the HPSCI release frames Finding 20 more specifically around contacts/support involving some hijackers and persons who may have connections to the Saudi Government, while the current GovInfo public rendering uses a more generalized formulation concerning potential foreign support. This is a **version/release difference**. It is not an evidentiary finding that either formulation proves the underlying allegations.

## Epistemic guard

Part Four itself preserves substantial caution. The Joint Inquiry public rendering states that it had not made final determinations about the reliability or sufficiency of the information and had not independently performed the extensive investigation necessary to determine the significance of alleged support.

The 2016 HPSCI release notice separately attributed to then-Chairman Devin Nunes the characterization that the section contained unverified leads rather than vetted conclusions. BlackIndex records that statement only as an attributed release-context statement, not as its own adjudication of every proposition in Part Four.

Therefore BlackIndex must distinguish:

`Joint Inquiry lead / reported information`

from:

`independently verified underlying fact`

and must separately track later FBI/CIA/Commission investigation of the same questions.
