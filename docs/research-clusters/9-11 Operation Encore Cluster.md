# 9/11 / Operation Encore — BlackIndex Research Cluster

## Purpose

Build a source-traceable evidence map for the Southern California support-network question, changing official assessments, and the investigative/accountability record surrounding Hazmi and Mihdhar.

This cluster does not adopt a final conclusion about any person or institution. It records:

- primary investigative records;
- official interpretations;
- negative findings with scope/access context;
- source dependencies;
- contradictions and corrections;
- missing or unreproduced underlying material;
- chronology of changing assessments.

## Negative-finding rule

The 2021 Operation Encore closure reported insufficient evidence to prosecute named subjects for witting conspiracy and reported completion of investigative steps. BlackIndex stores those statements as attributed FBI/DOJ findings.

Do not rewrite them as factual claims that no support, relationship, knowledge, or additional evidence existed outside the investigation's accessible record.

## Record-integrity priority

The September 2021 addendum corrected the original closing EC's claim of "hundreds" of interviews to approximately 60 interviews from 2007–2016. Preserve:

- original wording;
- corrected wording;
- reason for correction if documented;
- records used to quantify the correction;
- whether other closing-summary metrics were independently checked.

The correction is a concrete record-integrity event, not automatic proof of broader investigative unreliability.

## Source genealogy

Every repeated official claim should be traced backward to its underlying evidence. Commission, FBI, CIA and congressional reports may share common interviews, liaison reporting, financial records, telecommunications analysis or case serials.

Count derivative official repetition as one evidentiary lineage unless independently sourced evidence is identified.

## Key research questions

- What assistance to Hazmi and Mihdhar is directly documented?
- What did identified helpers know at the time?
- Which relationships to Saudi government, diplomatic, religious or business institutions are documented, and at what evidentiary level?
- Which early Joint Inquiry leads were later investigated, corroborated, narrowed, contradicted, or left unresolved?
- Which Operation Encore conclusions can be independently reproduced from released underlying records?
- What records were unavailable, withheld, redacted, destroyed, not sought, or remain unreleased?
- How did institutional assessments change over time and why?

## Hard rule

No early lead becomes fact because Congress published it. No later investigative closure becomes factual absence because the FBI wrote it. The evidence map preserves both and the record between them.
