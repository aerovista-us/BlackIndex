# ROTHSCHILD / FINANCE & INTELLIGENCE

## Research posture

This cluster is an evidence-map investigation, not a family-level allegation.

BlackIndex should preserve specific assertions about named people, institutions, historical events, intelligence relationships, financial networks, and official investigations without converting proximity, association, inherited surname, or repeated online claims into guilt or fact.

For this cluster in particular, **source genealogy is mandatory**. Many Rothschild-related narratives are repeated across books, websites, political literature, intelligence memoirs, and antisemitic conspiracy material. Twenty derivative repetitions of one unsupported or weak source remain one evidentiary lineage.

For every material assertion, trace:

`earliest available primary source → direct reproductions → later official interpretation → independent analysis → derivative retellings`

Count genuinely independent evidence separately; do not count repetition as corroboration.

Known starting record:
- `KV 2/4703` / `PF 604582 / V4`
- https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/release-2025-01/kv2-4703.pdf

Research questions:
- What does the personnel/security file actually establish about Victor Rothschild's relationship with MI5?

## Core guardrail

Victor Rothschild's proximity to genuine Cambridge espionage cases makes the documentary record worth investigating. It does not itself establish that allegations about him were true.

BlackIndex should be able to preserve, indefinitely and side-by-side:

- documented association
- official suspicion
- exculpatory or negative investigative findings
- contradictory evidence
- missing evidence
- source dependence
- unresolved questions

without forcing a family-wide or individual final verdict.
