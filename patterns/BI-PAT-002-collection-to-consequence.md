# BI-PAT-002 — Collection-to-consequence chain

## Definition
Risk is created not only by collecting information or generating an event, but by the downstream chain that attributes meaning, disseminates material, repurposes it, and converts it into consequential action.

## Evidence base
- Operation Northwoods: proposed incidents were paired with attribution and public/international narrative effects intended to support intervention.
- COINTELPRO Alexandria: collected information was considered for press exposure, landlord pressure, legal pressure, and other disruptive use.
- Church Committee Book II: the Senate review emphasizes cumulative risks across collection, dissemination, retention, and covert action.

## Indicators
- Intelligence/security data exported into unrelated adverse-action systems.
- Event generation and attribution controlled by the same operational actor.
- Consequences triggered from unverified or context-stripped information.
- No audit trail linking source evidence to downstream action.

## Controls
- End-to-end provenance.
- Separate authorization for materially different secondary uses.
- Independent attribution/evidence review.
- Full-chain audit from source through action and disposition.

## Confidence
**High** across three reviewed sources.

## Guardrail
Truth or authenticity of the underlying information does not by itself establish authority for every downstream use.
