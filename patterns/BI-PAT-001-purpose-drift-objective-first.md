# BI-PAT-001 — Purpose drift / objective-first justification

## Definition
A desired intervention, sanction, or policy outcome is selected before neutral evidence review is complete, causing collection or analysis to drift toward producing the predicate needed for that outcome.

## Evidence base
- Operation Northwoods: proposed pretexts were developed specifically to justify a desired military intervention.
- COINTELPRO Alexandria: investigative information was evaluated for disruptive or adverse secondary use.
- Church Committee Book II: oversight findings document domestic intelligence expansion beyond narrow predicates and resulting rights/accountability risks.

## Indicators
- Outcome language precedes evidence in planning records.
- Teams search for exploitable facts after a target/action has already been selected.
- Secondary use of collected information lacks a new authority/purpose decision.
- `No action` outcomes are structurally disfavored.

## Controls
- Independent predicate review.
- Explicit purpose-binding and secondary-use approval.
- Outcome-neutral performance metrics.
- Mandatory alternative-hypothesis review for high-impact actions.

## Confidence
**High** across three reviewed sources.

## Guardrail
This pattern identifies a governance mechanism. It does not prove that organizations or events sharing the mechanism are historically connected.
