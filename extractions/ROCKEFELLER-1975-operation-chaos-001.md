## CLAIM
The Rockefeller Commission investigated CIA activities within the United States and described Operation CHAOS as an effort that began with a foreign-influence purpose but developed domestic aspects significant enough to require formal review.

## DOCUMENT CONTENT
The report traces the program's growth, staffing, compartmentation, tasking, relationship with the FBI, and senior-level attention. It records that CIA leadership emphasized statutory restrictions on domestic activities while the program simultaneously expanded collection and support activity around the domestic radical milieu.
