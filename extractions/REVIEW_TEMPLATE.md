## CLAIM

What assertion, allegation, proposal, observation, conclusion, or research question is being evaluated?

## DOCUMENT CONTENT

Neutral description of what is actually present in the record.

## SOURCE ATTRIBUTION

Who is making the assertion? Record role, institution, date, firsthand/retrospective status, and relevant source/conflict information.

## CORROBORATION

List material supporting the assertion.

For each source note whether it is **independent** or **derivative** of another source already counted.

## CONFLICTS

List evidence, testimony, records, versions, or official statements inconsistent with or materially qualifying the assertion.

Do not force a resolution where the record does not support one.

## GAPS

List missing, destroyed, withheld, redacted, unavailable, never-requested, or otherwise inaccessible material relevant to this claim.

Use `MISSING_EVIDENCE` records for material gaps.

## ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

Strongest reasonable mundane, innocent, procedural, or competing explanation for anomalies or apparently suspicious features.

## UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS

What specific records, versions, testimony, technical evidence, chain-of-custody information, or archival material would materially improve understanding?

## SOURCE

Primary-document citation with page/exhibit/message number and archival identifier.

Use exact quotation + page citation for pivotal assertions that materially influence scores, patterns, contradiction findings, or record-integrity findings.

# Evidence layers

## Primary document

Describe/link the underlying artifact only.

## Official interpretation

Record agency/congressional/investigative descriptions separately and attribute them.

## Independent analysis

Record BlackIndex/external analysis separately from both the artifact and official interpretation.
