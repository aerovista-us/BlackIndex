## CLAIM
TPAJAX was an implemented CIA project with dedicated financial administration and obligations, not merely a retrospective label or hypothetical plan.

## DOCUMENT SAYS
The Finance Division memorandum explicitly refers to project TPAJAX, project allotments, obligations, advances, expenditures, and administrative discussion of how project funds should be recorded. The memorandum concerns financial treatment after planning and implementation activity had occurred.

This is direct documentary evidence that TPAJAX existed as an operationally administered project. It is a narrow but important provenance record linking the project name to CIA financial and administrative machinery.

## DOES NOT PROVE
- This two-page finance record does not by itself establish the full sequence of political, propaganda, military, British, royal, or street-level actions associated with the 1953 overthrow of Mossadegh.
- It does not prove that every proposed TPAJAX activity was executed.
- Sanitized names and amounts prevent full reconstruction from this record alone.

## ANOMALIES
- Multiple names, amounts, and locations are sanitized.
- The surviving record focuses on accounting treatment rather than operational narrative.
- Financial terminology can establish implementation/capability but not necessarily the exact action funded.
