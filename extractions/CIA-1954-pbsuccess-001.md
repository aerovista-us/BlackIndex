## CLAIM
PBSUCCESS was a CIA covert program whose stated objectives included removing the Árbenz government and covertly creating political conditions favorable to a pro-U.S. successor government.

## DOCUMENT SAYS
The memorandum explicitly restates PBSUCCESS general objectives, including covert removal of the then Guatemalan government and installation/sustainment of a pro-U.S. government. It then defines Stage Two political/psychological objectives including creating dissension and defection within the target, discrediting the target domestically and abroad, and shaping political attitudes.

This is direct documentary evidence of objective-first covert-action planning and a structured political/psychological campaign component.

## DOES NOT PROVE
- The memo alone does not establish that every listed technique was executed.
- It does not by itself establish the full military, diplomatic, propaganda, or assassination-related history of PBSUCCESS.
- It does not prove that every political change in Guatemala during the period resulted from CIA action.

## ANOMALIES
- Operational identities and some details remain sanitized in the wider PBSUCCESS corpus.
- Planning documents can overstate intended control over real-world outcomes.
- Objective language should be separated from subsequent execution and outcome evidence.
