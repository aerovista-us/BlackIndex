## CLAIM

This April 2026 first-party Navy budget justification provides a later official program state for E-130J / TACAMO Modernization PE `0605180N`. Compared with the June 2025 FY2026 BA5 submission, it documents revised funding and a materially extended current program schedule.

The source does **not** by itself establish that Preliminary Design Review, aircraft builds, mission-system integration, CSIL testing, Integrated Test 1, SDTA delivery, retrofit, fleet acceptance, IOC/FOC, readiness, or mission employment actually occurred.

## DOCUMENT CONTENT

### Funding state

The FY2027 book lists:

- FY2025: **$727.931 million**
- FY2026: **$1,043.978 million**
- FY2027 base request: **$1,662.723 million**

The Program Change Summary shows the FY2026 amount declining from the prior President's Budget figure of **$1,243.978 million** to **$1,043.978 million**, with a **$200.000 million Congressional Directed Reduction**.

For FY2027, the source shows a prior President's Budget figure of **$2,107.158 million** and a current request of **$1,662.723 million**, a net adjustment of **-$444.435 million**. The narrative attributes $443.275 million of that decrease to higher Navy priorities and $1.160 million to rate/miscellaneous adjustments.

The source explicitly says: **“The R3 schedule has been updated to reflect the current program schedule.”**

### FY2026 / FY2027 work descriptions

The FY2027 submission describes FY2026 funding as procuring startup materials for SDTA C-130J-30 Government Furnished Equipment and continuing EMD design/integration work.

FY2027 funding is described as purchasing **three C-130J-30 SDTA aircraft** for mission-system integration and continuing EMD. The top-level justification also refers to integration of three Engineering Development Model aircraft.

For program-management support, the FY2026 plan text says work continues in preparation for **PDR and CDR**. The FY2027 base-plan text refers to preparation for **CDR**. This textual change is relevant to chronology, but BlackIndex does not infer PDR completion from the omission of PDR in the later plan text.

### Current schedule versus June 2025 schedule

The June 2025 FY2026 R-4A and April 2026 FY2027 R-4A differ materially:

- E-130J EMD air-vehicle build: **Q1 FY2024–Q3 FY2026** → **Q1 FY2025–Q1 FY2027**
- AV1 mission-system integration modification: **Q3–Q4 FY2026** → **Q1 FY2027–Q3 FY2028**
- Mission System 2 aircraft build: **Q1 FY2024–Q3 FY2026** → **Q1 FY2025–Q1 FY2027**
- MS2 mission-system integration modification: **Q3–Q4 FY2026** → **Q1 FY2027–Q2 FY2029**
- Mission System 3 aircraft build: **Q1 FY2024–Q3 FY2026** → **Q1 FY2025–Q1 FY2027**
- MS3 mission-system integration modification: **Q3–Q4 FY2026** → **Q1 FY2027–Q2 FY2029**
- SDTA C-130J-30 GFP build: **Q1–Q4 FY2026** → **Q2 FY2026–Q2 FY2029**
- CSIL Test: **Q3–Q4 FY2026** → **Q1 FY2027–Q4 FY2031**
- Integrated Test 1: **Q2–Q4 FY2026** → **Q1 FY2027–Q2 FY2028**
- E-130J TACAMO Weapons System EMD contract window: **Q1 FY2025–Q4 FY2026** → **Q1 FY2025–Q4 FY2031**

The April 2026 schedule also adds later milestones including CDR, Milestone C, Government STIL Test, Operational Assessments, and Integrated Tests 2 and 3.

The later budget therefore supports a **schedule-revision finding**, not a blanket completion or failure finding.
