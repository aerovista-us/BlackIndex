# NSA — Skunks, Bogies, Silent Hounds, and the Flying Fish

- **Doc ID:** `NSA-2001-gulf-of-tonkin-001`
- **Call ID:** `CALL-GULF-TONKIN`
- **Evidence status:** reviewed

## CLAIM
NSA's later cryptologic reconstruction argues that SIGINT used to support the 4 August 1964 attack narrative was mishandled and that other intercepted material pointed in a different direction.

## DOCUMENT CONTENT
The review reconstructs the 2–4 August reporting chain and compares raw/field SIGINT, summaries, later evaluations, and North Vietnamese traffic. It describes reporting that continued to support an attack narrative while identifying omitted, misread, or underweighted material. It highlights a 6 August North Vietnamese recap that mentions the 2 and 5 August actions but not a 4 August engagement, and asks why this intercept was not used in contemporary evaluations.

## SOURCE ATTRIBUTION
NSA Cryptologic Quarterly historical study. It is a later institutional/technical reconstruction, not a contemporaneous operational message.

## CORROBORATION
The study cites and reproduces numerous contemporaneous SIGINT and command records. Those underlying records should be ingested separately where practical so the retrospective analysis does not become the sole source for its own evidentiary chain.

## CONFLICTS
Contemporaneous operational reporting and the later JCS 'Proof of Attack' narrative contain observations interpreted as supporting an attack. The BlackIndex corpus should preserve both layers without forcing the retrospective review to overwrite the contemporaneous record.

## GAPS
- Some underlying intercepts, summaries, and processing records remain to be individually ingested.
- Institutional retrospective analysis can itself contain selection and interpretation choices.
- The current record does not yet reconstruct every information path from field collection to the President.

## ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS
Contemporaneous analysts may have interpreted ambiguous signals under severe time pressure and expectation of attack rather than intentionally manipulating the record. Later analysts benefit from hindsight and a broader corpus.

## UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS
- Which specific SIGINT items were available before the 4 August public address?
- Which items were withheld, delayed, mistranslated, or excluded from summaries?
- Can the full source-dependency graph of the attack narrative be reconstructed?
