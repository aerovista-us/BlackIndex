## Evidence established by the source set

1. The Pentagon Papers are the *Report of the Office of the Secretary of Defense Vietnam Task Force*, commissioned by Secretary of Defense Robert McNamara in 1967.
2. NARA states that the complete archival release is approximately 7,000 pages in 48 boxes and that about 34% was made available for the first time in the 2011 complete release.
3. NARA states that this release is complete and unredacted relative to previous public versions, is arranged as Leslie Gelb presented it to Secretary of Defense Clark Clifford on 15 January 1969, and includes supplemental back-documentation omitted from earlier editions.
4. The index maps the report into major analytic categories including historical background, evolution of the war, public justifications, internal justification documents, and settlement/negotiation records.
5. The index is therefore an archival navigation document. It does not by itself establish the substantive claims contained in the underlying volumes.
