# JCS — Proof of Attack, Gulf of Tonkin

- **Doc ID:** `JCS-1964-gulf-of-tonkin-001`
- **Call ID:** `CALL-GULF-TONKIN`
- **Evidence status:** reviewed

## CLAIM
A 7 August 1964 JCS/CINCPACFLT message assembled operational reports and witness statements as proof supporting the existence of a 4 August attack.

## DOCUMENT CONTENT
The message recounts radar contacts, reported torpedo indications, visual observations, searchlight sightings, and witness descriptions. It states that the narrative was derived from operational summaries and statements. It also records that there was no U.S. damage during the 4 August action and no photography obtained during the night action.

## SOURCE ATTRIBUTION
CINCPACFLT/JCS message traffic preserved through the Johnson Library and NSA historical-release collection.

## CORROBORATION
The message cites multiple shipboard and airborne observers and operational summaries.

## CONFLICTS
Later NSA reconstruction questions the interpretation and handling of SIGINT supporting the second attack and identifies intercepted material inconsistent with it. The absence of physical damage or photography is relevant context but is not independently dispositive.

## GAPS
- Underlying witness statements are not contained in this three-page message.
- Sensor logs and raw radar/sonar data are not reproduced here.
- The message does not independently test whether reported observations were mutually dependent or influenced by common expectations.

## ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS
Some reported observations could reflect genuine contacts; others could reflect weather, sensor effects, misidentification, or crisis-driven interpretation. The document itself does not resolve those possibilities.

## UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS
- Which cited observations can be independently reconstructed from raw logs?
- Were witness statements taken before or after exposure to the developing attack narrative?
- Which SIGINT reports available on 7 August were included or excluded from the synthesis?

Johnson Library / NSA historical release, `19640807_1960_DOC_JOHNSONLIBRARY_JCS.PDF`, pages 1–3.
