## CLAIM
CIA's later institutional history describes the planning and implementation of TPAJAX and the role of U.S. personnel, Iranian political actors, propaganda, the Shah's decrees, and the failed first attempt followed by continued activity.

## DOCUMENT CONTENT
The CIA historical account places TPAJAX inside the Agency's institutional history and describes a planned effort to remove Mossadegh, including efforts to obtain royal decrees and organize political/operational support. It treats the failed 15–16 August phase and subsequent events as part of the same operational sequence.

## SOURCE ATTRIBUTION
CIA institutional history written decades after the operation. It is not a contemporaneous cable or order and should remain separate from raw TPAJAX operational records.

## CORROBORATION
The later FRUS Iran volume includes contemporaneous CIA/NSC records describing capabilities, authorization, implementation phases, operational failure, propaganda, covert military apparatus, costs, and the later assessment that the operation succeeded. President Eisenhower's diary also records U.S. covert involvement.

## CONFLICTS
Retrospective institutional histories can compress disputed operational details, omit source-level uncertainty, or rely on records unavailable to the reader. Individual causal claims therefore require comparison with contemporaneous cables and the FRUS source apparatus.
